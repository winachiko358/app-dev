# 🚗 My Favorite Movie: *Cars*

## 🏁 Overview
**Cars** is a 2006 animated film produced by *Pixar Animation Studios* and released by *Walt Disney Pictures*.  
It tells the story of **Lightning McQueen**, a rookie race car who learns that life is about the journey, not just the finish line.

---

## 🌟 Why I Love It
1. **Inspiring Message** – It teaches humility and teamwork.  
2. *Amazing Animation* – The details of Radiator Springs and the racetrack are stunning.  
3. **Memorable Characters** – Mater, Doc Hudson, and Sally make the story unforgettable.  

> “Float like a Cadillac, sting like a Beemer.”
